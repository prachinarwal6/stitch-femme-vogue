## Brand & Style

This design system channels the refined elegance of high-fashion print magazines infused with the fluidity and responsiveness of a modern luxury boutique. It blends timeless editorial sophistication with a fresh, youthful vitality, delivering an experience that feels aspirational, curated, and warm.

### Design Principles
- **Editorial Poise:** Layouts treat digital screens like dynamic glossies. Asymmetrical grids, expansive white space, and commanding display serifs establish an effortless visual hierarchy.
- **Warm Tactility:** Surfaces reject sterile tech minimalism. Instead, they lean into warm ivory backdrops, champagne gold touches, and translucent rose blurs.
- **Sensory Refinement:** Fine hairline borders, diffused ambient golden glows, and gentle curves balance classical haute-couture structure with tactile modern interaction.
- **Uncompromised Legibility:** High-contrast display typography is anchored by crisp, highly legible geometric body typography with intentional tracking on micro-labels and editorial metadata.

## Colors

The palette takes inspiration from golden champagne, blushing silk, and heavy matte archive paper. The contrast between warm alabaster backdrops and saturated noir ink creates an unmistakable editorial atmosphere.

### Primary Accents (Champagne Gold)
- **Primary Base (`#D4AF37`):** Applied to active indicators, luxury badges, prominent call-to-actions, and focal accents.
- **Deep Champagne (`#C5A059`):** Reserved for interactive states, borders on high-contrast surfaces, and hover treatments for primary elements.

### Secondary Accents (Blush & Rose)
- **Rose Mauve (`#E8A598`):** Provides a warm, romantic secondary accent for notifications, highlights, and secondary tags.
- **Blush Frost (`#F7D6D0`, `#FCEEE9`):** Used for soft tinted pill containers, subtle card backdrops, and active toggle fills.

### Neutrals & Text Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF7F2`):** Primary page background tint emulating heavy unbleached editorial stock.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** High-key card surface for crisp separation against the canvas.
- **Surface Muted (`#F5F0EB`):** Subtle container background for chips, input fields, and recessed modules.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#EADCD5`, `#E5DFD7`):** Hairline separation lines providing structure without heaviness.
- **Headline / Noir Text (`#121212`):** Primary text color delivering sharp, ink-like contrast.
- **Secondary Charcoal (`#1E1E1E`):** Secondary text for lead paragraphs and primary controls.
- **Muted Mauve (`#7A6B69`):** Tertiary captions, dates, authors, and metadata.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-fashion grandeur of Playfair Display with the geometric clarity of Plus Jakarta Sans. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in Playfair Display. Designed to mimic luxury mastheads, opening article flourishes, and boutique collections. Letter-spacing is slightly contracted on large display treatments for typographic density and visual authority.
- **Body Text:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans. Line height is purposefully relaxed (1.6 to 1.65) to maximize readability across dense editorial content.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Set in uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans with generous letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`). This treatment is strictly applied to categories, section flags, product tags, button labels, and timestamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adopts a fluid 12-column editorial grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single- or two-column flow on mobile viewports.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` of 1.25rem (20px), and `gutter-mobile` of 1rem (16px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, 2rem margins, and 1.25rem gutters.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, max-width constrained to 1440px with auto centring, `margin` of 2rem (32px), and `gutter` of 1.5rem (24px).

### Editorial Spacing Rhythm
Sections use dramatic macro-spacing (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) to create expansive editorial pauses between featured spreads, product curations, and lookbooks. Micro-spacing (`space-xs` through `space-md`) ensures tightly grouped captions, tags, and price badges remain contextually tied to their parent imagery.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft ambient diffusion and frosted layers rather than heavy mechanical drops.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FAF7F2` baseline background. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a subtle hairline border (`1px solid #EADCD5`) and an ultra-diffused warm ambient shadow: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(122, 107, 105, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Quickviews, & Floating Menus):** Pure white surface elevated with `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.08)` and subtle border styling.
- **Glassmorphism (Overlays & Navigation Bars):** Frosted glass surfaces using `background: rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a fine bottom edge border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).
- **Champagne Highlight Glow:** Subtle golden focus rings and hover glows utilize `0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` to signify active states with luxury warmth.

## Shapes

The design system uses a balanced rounded geometry (level 2). 

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Inputs, standard buttons, dropdown lists, and minor UI controls.
- **Editorial Cards & Containers (1rem / 16px to 1.5rem / 24px):** Article cards, product display units, and modal sheets use `rounded-lg` and `rounded-xl` to soften photography framing without feeling cartoonish.
- **Pill Shapes (Full Round):** Exclusively reserved for status badges, category tags, author avatars, and floating navigation pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Editorial:** Background in deep noir `#121212`, text in pure white `#FFFFFF`, uppercase `label-md` with `0.08em` letter spacing. Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Corner radius: `0.5rem`. Hover transitions smoothly to champagne gold `#D4AF37`.
- **Secondary Luxury:** Outlined with `1px solid #121212`, transparent background, dark text. Hover state fills with warm blush `#FCEEE9` with a gold accent border `#C5A059`.
- **Champagne Accent:** Solid `#D4AF37` fill with dark text `#121212` for standout e-commerce actions (e.g., "Add to Bag"). Hover darkens to `#C5A059`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Category Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), background `#FCEEE9`, text `#7A6B69`, font `label-sm`.
- **Gold Accent Tags:** Semi-transparent gold tint (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`), text `#C5A059`, bordered with `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`.

### Form Fields (Input, Checkbox, Radio)
- **Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F0EB`, border `1px solid #EADCD5`, text `#121212`, placeholder `#7A6B69`. Focus state applies border `#D4AF37` and warm halo `0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`. Corner radius `0.5rem`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unchecked border `1.5px solid #EADCD5`. Checked fill `#121212` with champagne `#D4AF37` check icon / center radio dot.

### Cards
- **Article & Lookbook Card:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface, corner radius `1rem`, framed by a fine border `1px solid #EADCD5`. Photography inside maintains a 4:5 or 3:4 aspect ratio with `rounded-md` internal margins. Hover elevates with smooth translateY (-4px) and warm ambient glow.
- **Product Boutique Card:** Borderless clean white background floating on ivory canvas, centered editorial typography, gold price tags, and interactive quick-shop triggers.

### Custom Components: Editorial Quote & Lookbook Carousel
- **Editorial Pull Quote:** High-contrast `Playfair Display` italic text anchored by a left accent rule in `#D4AF37` (2px width), accompanied by metadata in uppercase muted mauve.
- **Story Banner Pill:** A floating sticky bar at screen edges housing collection progress or reading time, encased in frosted glass with gold border detailing.
