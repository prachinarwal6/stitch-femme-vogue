The Neo-Minimalist Trench: Sculpted Shoulders & Floor-Sweeping Wool

Moving away from fleeting novelty, this season’s defining overcoat pairs brutalist shoulder pad geometry with feather-light, puddle-hem virgin wool. An uncompromising silhouette that commands city pavements while shielding without bulk.

“Architecture you can step into. The power coat has returned with an unapologetic fluid drape.”
— Clémence Roy, Paris Fashion Editor
